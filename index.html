TECTONIC EXCAVATION // CHAPTER 02—03
“WE DID NOT BUILD ON THE CLIFF.
WE BLUDGEONED 24,000 TONS OF GNEISS UNTIL THE ROCK HAD NO CHOICE BUT TO BECOME VAULT AND CEILING.”
01 // 14M CANTILEVER

180 tons of basalt concrete hung over sheer air. Thirty-two high-tensile steel tendon cables torqued to 850 kilonewtons. Deflection tolerance is mathematically zero.

02 // ROCK FRICTION

Three 300-meter vertical borehole shafts drilled through active alpine fissures. Friction-locked into the continental crust like an iron tooth.

03 // SEISMIC HYDRAULICS

Raw diamond-wire cut granite slabs mated to sub-millimeter tolerances on three hydro-pneumatic dampeners rated for Category 9 tectonic rupture.
